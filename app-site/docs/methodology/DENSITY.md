# Population density methodology

Population density is a derived observation:

`population / land area`

A valid density record must identify:

- population observation;
- land-area or boundary record;
- geography;
- reference period;
- boundary version;
- source release or calculation;
- unit;
- formula version;
- quality and preferred status.

Density is an average over a declared territory. It is not a claim that people are distributed evenly in physical space.
