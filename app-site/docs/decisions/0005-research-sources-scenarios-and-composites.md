# ADR 0005: Separate research sources, scenarios and composite geographies

## Status

Accepted for Release 0.1.

## Decision

Videos, conversations and articles may be recorded as research sources without becoming statistical datasets or verified evidence.

Historical thought experiments are stored as scenarios. Source-asserted numbers remain unverified inputs until linked to canonical observations.

Constructed areas such as “Texas + Oklahoma + Louisiana” are geographic composites with explicit members, not ordinary places.

## Consequences

Metroplist can preserve the conceptual lineage of an experience while independently fact-checking every place, boundary, period and value used to reproduce or update it.
