# Place identity

Names and URLs are aliases, not primary identity.

Every canonical place receives an immutable Metroplist identifier. Official identifiers from ONS or another authority are retained as external identifiers with validity periods and source releases.

A single search term may resolve to several entities. “Greenwich”, “London”, “Tokyo” and “Manila” must not be silently collapsed into one assumed geography.

A place persists conceptually while its administrative or statistical geographies and boundaries may change over time.
