# Scenarios, anchors and composite geographies

A scenario is not an observation.

The 2016 “everyone in one city” thought experiment uses a source-asserted population snapshot and pairs density templates with footprint references. Those inputs are preserved as a historical media scenario until they are independently linked to canonical observations.

## Anchor roles

- **Density reference** — a place or historical geography whose density is used as a template.
- **Footprint reference** — a territory used to visualise the land required under that template.
- **Scale/context anchor** — a park, metropolitan area or nested geography used to explain scale.
- **Local Metroplist anchor** — a verified place selected for the first operational comparison flow.

## Composite geography

“Texas + Oklahoma + Louisiana” is not a natural single place. It is a constructed composite with declared members and a source-defined purpose. Composite records must never silently masquerade as administrative areas.

## Ambiguous labels

Tokyo, London, Manila and Palestine require a declared geographic definition before any population, area or density value is attached.
